## Artwork
- `assets/characters/*.svg`, `assets/brand/*.svg`: original to IconMorph, CC0-1.0 / MIT respectively (see `assets/manifests/assets.json`).
- Emoji are **not bundled**. They are drawn at runtime by the user's installed system emoji font.
- `public/collections/pixel/*.svg` (Pixel Friends) and `public/collections/minimal/*.svg` (Minimalist): original, generated by `scripts/gen-collections.py`, CC0-1.0.
